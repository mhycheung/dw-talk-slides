$\sim e^{-im\Omega_H u}\,$$\Big[$$\color{#ee4266}c_1\epsilon$${}+{}\color{#ff8c00}c_2\epsilon^2$${}+{}\color{#2a9d8f}c_3\epsilon^3$${}+{}\color{#3a86ff}c_4\epsilon^4$${}+{}\color{#8338ec}c_5\epsilon^5$${}+{}\cdots$$\Big]$

Mino+ (0809.2814)

Horizon modes are screened

  • Zimmerman+ (1106.0782): $\omega_H^{(1)}$ and $\omega_H^{(2)}$ screened by a factor in front of the source term, to leading order in $\epsilon$, but $\omega\to\omega_H^{(2)}$ at next order
  • Oshita+ (2509.09165), stationary phase approximation (SPA): $\omega_H^{(n\ge3)}$ are also screened by a factor in front of $G(r)$, but $\omega\to\omega_H^{(2)}$
  • Kuntz+ (2606.07173): SPA is problematic; in Schwarzschild all $\omega_H^{(n)}$ have zero amplitude, by causality
  • Ma+ (2607.06677): no SPA, avoids the problem in Kuntz+; the $\omega_H^{(1)}$ pieces cancel in the sum over filter poles, but $\omega\approx\omega_G$
  • Kubota+ (2609.07326): SPA is problematic; all $\omega_H^{(n)}$ screened by the prefactors in front of $G(r)$ and $S(r)$, so none survives
  • Sun+ (2608.23209): all $\omega_H^{(n)}$ screened, but the instantaneous $\omega\to\omega_H^{(3)}$
